  1. Play the song and tap on every beat, as you would tap your foot. Slower playback makes fast songs easier.
  2. Taps land a little late (the hand, the keyboard). Turn the click on, play again and move every beat with the slider until the clicks sit exactly on the beats.
  3. Save: the file is named after the song (song.beats.txt). Put it next to the song in one folder (for example real/), then run node bench.mjs real.
